# USA251

### The Next Chapter Is Ours

**New Horizons. Big Dreams. Real Work. Better Lives.**

USA251 is an independent civic and cultural initiative exploring America's extraordinary journey, the people who shaped it, and the possibilities that lie ahead.

As America enters its 251st year, we have an opportunity to look beyond commemoration and consider what we can accomplish together.

The project brings history, culture, community celebrations, and forward-looking exhibits into one accessible platform.

### What We're Building

- **An Extraordinary Journey:** Exploring American history through the people, events, and ideas that shaped the nation.
- **The American Fair:** Celebrating the many cultures, traditions, and communities that make up America.
- **251 and the Next 250:** Exploring practical possibilities for improving American life, from healthcare and education to transportation and beyond.

### Project Status

USA251 is in its initial development phase. Content, exhibits, and opportunities for participation will expand as the project takes shape.

**Website:** https://USA251.org

### Our Perspective

History is not simply something to remember. It is something we inherit, participate in, and continue to create.

**The next chapter is ours.**
