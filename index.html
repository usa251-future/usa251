Come HungryWisconsin

A Friday night worth keeping

Wisconsin’s fish fry

A Friday fish fry can mean perch or walleye, potato pancakes, rye bread, and a room full of neighbors. Religious custom, freshwater fish and local taverns became a tradition that people still organize their Fridays around.

TraditionOpen exhibit →
Come HungryLouisiana

Crawfish, Cajun music and three days together

Breaux Bridge Crawfish Festival

In Breaux Bridge, crawfish is both a food and a reason to gather. Cajun and zydeco bands join cooks, families, and visitors for a festival rooted in the region’s culture. The organizer lists April 30–May 2 for 2027.

Our TraditionsUtah

Spring arrives in a cloud of color

Holi Festival of Colors

At the Radha Krishna Temple in Spanish Fork, Holi becomes a public celebration of spring: music, dancing, shared food and great clouds of color. Visitors from many backgrounds join the festivities.

Annual traditionOpen exhibit →
Our TraditionsCalifornia

Tết comes to Little Saigon

UVSA Tết Festival

In Orange County, Vietnamese New Year fills a cultural village with food, performances, games and family traditions. Student and community organizers create an occasion to carry heritage forward and share it widely.

2027 dates postedOpen exhibit →
Turn It UpArkansas / National

The electric guitar had a gospel pioneer

Sister Rosetta Tharpe

Before rock and roll had its familiar stars, Sister Rosetta Tharpe was playing astonishing guitar and bringing gospel energy to new audiences. Her influence reached musicians who transformed American popular music.

American musicOpen exhibit →
Turn It UpMississippi

A whole nation on seven stages

National Folk Festival

Blues, klezmer, hip-hop, zydeco, polka, gospel and more share space at the National Folk Festival. Jackson, Mississippi, is its host city through 2027, with free performances and traditional arts.

2027 host confirmedOpen exhibit →
Around the BlockMichigan

One kid became thousands

Jimmy’s Kids, Detroit

Jim Tuman began helping children in Detroit in 1988. Jimmy’s Kids grew into a volunteer effort delivering toys, food and other help to children and families. One person’s initiative became an enduring community tradition.

Ongoing organizationOpen exhibit →
Around the BlockMinnesota

A neighborhood builds its own parade

Mayday Minneapolis

South Minneapolis makes giant puppets, music, art and a springtime celebration in garages, schools and backyards. The community-led Mayday Parade and festival have roots reaching back to 1975.

Annual traditionOpen exhibit →
Made HereVirginia

The people who calculated spaceflight

NASA’s Hidden Figures

Katherine Johnson, Dorothy Vaughan, Mary Jackson and their colleagues supplied the mathematical work that made early American space missions possible. Their careers opened doors in science and engineering.

Historic achievementOpen exhibit →
Made HereOklahoma / California

Mary Golda Ross mapped possibilities

A Cherokee aerospace engineer

Mary Golda Ross, a member of the Cherokee Nation, became a pioneering aerospace engineer. Her work at Lockheed included early spaceflight planning, bringing extraordinary technical skill to the dawn of the space age.
