250 Years. Millions of Stories. One Nation.

The founding ideas. The people who challenged them. The communities, inventions, movements and decisions that shaped our lives. This history belongs to every American, including the stories that happened close to home.

Before 1776

The story begins long before independence.

Indigenous nations governed, traded, farmed, created and sustained communities across North America for thousands of years. Colonization brought major upheaval, resistance and exchange. These histories shaped the country that emerged in 1776 and remain part of the American story today.

A Connected, Contested Century

A new century brought crisis, innovation, shifting demographics, new social movements and new questions about the country we want to share.

  • 2001
    September 11 attacks reshape national security and public life. Source ↗
  • 2003
    Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) is established under the new Department of Homeland Security. Source ↗
  • 2008
    ICE arrests 389 workers at the Agriprocessors plant in Postville, Iowa, in a raid that reshapes community debates about enforcement and due process. Source ↗
  • 2015
    Obergefell v. Hodges establishes a constitutional right to same-sex marriage. Source ↗
  • 2018
    The federal zero-tolerance policy produces widespread family separations at the southern border; DHS inspectors document failures in tracking and reunification. The policy involved multiple DHS agencies, including CBP and ICE. Source ↗
  • 2019
    ICE arrests approximately 680 workers at seven Mississippi poultry plants, one of the largest workplace enforcement actions of the period. Source ↗
  • 2020
    The COVID-19 pandemic tests healthcare, schools, families and civic institutions. Source ↗
  • 2025
    Large immigration enforcement operations in Los Angeles and Chicago trigger protests, legal disputes and intense debate over public safety and civil liberties. Source ↗
  • 2026
    Operation Metro Surge in Minnesota becomes a national flashpoint after broad enforcement, community protests and the fatal shootings of Renee Good and Alex Pretti by federal officers. Source ↗
